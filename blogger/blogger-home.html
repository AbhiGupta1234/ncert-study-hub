Everything you need to ace Class 10, in one free app.

NCERT Study Hub is a desktop study companion for CBSE Class 10 — chapter-wise NCERT textbooks, an attendance calendar, syllabus progress tracking and an AI tutor. Works fully offline after install.

Free · No account on our servers · Windows ~245 MB · Android ~3 MB

Built for real study sessions

Five subjects, every chapter, zero distractions. Your progress stays on your device and syncs to your own Google Drive if you want it to.

Chapter PDFs, built in

Official NCERT textbooks for all five Class 10 subjects ship with the app. Open any chapter instantly — no internet needed.

Attendance calendar

Mark school days as present or absent and see monthly summaries at a glance, so you always know where you stand.

AI tutor

Ask doubts in plain language. Bring your own OpenAI-compatible API key — chat history is saved privately under your account.

Syllabus dashboard

Tick chapters off as you finish them and watch subject-wise completion fill up. Perfect for board-exam planning.

Google Drive backup

Sign in with Google to back up your progress and chats to your own Drive. Optional — everything works without it too.

Private by design

No telemetry, no ads, no accounts on our side. Your data lives locally on your device, organised under your email.
